# 历史资料重建：SMSN [2025Q1](https://news.samsung.com/global/samsung-electronics-announces-fourth-quarter-and-fy-2024-results) 财报前瞻

以下基于三星电子官方上季发布，仅作历史资料重建，不包含本季实际结果。

## 上季回顾
三星电子公布上季（[2024Q4](https://news.samsung.com/global/samsung-electronics-announces-fourth-quarter-and-fy-2024-results)）合并营收 [75.8 万亿韩元](https://news.samsung.com/global/samsung-electronics-announces-fourth-quarter-and-fy-2024-results)，合并营业利润 [6.5 万亿韩元](https://news.samsung.com/global/samsung-electronics-announces-fourth-quarter-and-fy-2024-results)。整体看，上季业务规模与利润仍处高位，但该数据为合并口径；前瞻本季时，应关注各业务组合、价格与费用变化，而非简单线性外推。

## 本季验证问题
- 验证问题一：上季 [75.8 万亿韩元](https://news.samsung.com/global/samsung-electronics-announces-fourth-quarter-and-fy-2024-results) 的合并营收基数，本季能否靠出货、产品组合或汇率等因素获得支撑？
- 验证问题二：上季 [6.5 万亿韩元](https://news.samsung.com/global/samsung-electronics-announces-fourth-quarter-and-fy-2024-results) 的营业利润基数，本季成本控制、库存与一次性项目会否造成明显偏离？

## 正负情形
- 正面情形：若需求与产品组合改善、成本纪律延续，本季合并营收有机会维持在上季 [75.8 万亿韩元](https://news.samsung.com/global/samsung-electronics-announces-fourth-quarter-and-fy-2024-results) 附近，营业利润也可能接近上季 [6.5 万亿韩元](https://news.samsung.com/global/samsung-electronics-announces-fourth-quarter-and-fy-2024-results) 量级。
- 负面情形：若价格压力、库存调整或费用上升超预期，本季合并营收可能低于上季 [75.8 万亿韩元](https://news.samsung.com/global/samsung-electronics-announces-fourth-quarter-and-fy-2024-results)，营业利润也可能弱于上季 [6.5 万亿韩元](https://news.samsung.com/global/samsung-electronics-announces-fourth-quarter-and-fy-2024-results)。
