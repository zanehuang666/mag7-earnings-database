# 历史资料重建｜SKHX [2025Q3](https://news.skhynix.com/en/sk-hynix-announces-2q25-financial-results/) 财报前瞻

上季（[2Q25](https://news.skhynix.com/en/sk-hynix-announces-2q25-financial-results/)）SK hynix 在 AI 内存需求下实现营收 [KRW 22.232 万亿](https://news.skhynix.com/en/sk-hynix-announces-2q25-financial-results/)、营业利润 [KRW 9.2129 万亿](https://news.skhynix.com/en/sk-hynix-announces-2q25-financial-results/)、净利润 [KRW 6.9962 万亿](https://news.skhynix.com/en/sk-hynix-announces-2q25-financial-results/)，营业利润率 [41%](https://news.skhynix.com/en/sk-hynix-announces-2q25-financial-results/)。[HBM3E](https://news.skhynix.com/en/sk-hynix-announces-2q25-financial-results/) 与 NAND 销售扩张是主要驱动，盈利结构明显改善。前瞻本季，应重点验证 AI 内存拉货的持续性与产品组合升级能否延续，而非简单外推上季高点。

**验证问题一：** [HBM3E](https://news.skhynix.com/en/sk-hynix-announces-2q25-financial-results/) 出货与定价能否抵销传统内存波动，使营收与利润率维持高位？

**验证问题二：** NAND 需求扩张是否具备持续性，还是渠道补库的一次性效应？

正负情形：

- 正向：若 AI 服务器需求延续、[HBM3E](https://news.skhynix.com/en/sk-hynix-announces-2q25-financial-results/) 放量且 NAND 价格稳定，营收与营业利润率有望接近上季强势水平，利润弹性仍在。
- 负向：若客户调整库存、[HBM3E](https://news.skhynix.com/en/sk-hynix-announces-2q25-financial-results/) 竞争加剧或 NAND 价格回落，营收增速与利润率可能承压，营业利润较上季回落风险上升。
