# AMZN 2025Q4 财报分析

> 数据来源：Amazon IR 发布于2026-02-05；本稿只使用截至2026-02-05的已公开信息。关键数字链接：[Amazon.com Announces Fourth Quarter Results](https://ir.aboutamazon.com/news-release/news-release-details/2026/Amazon-com-Announces-Fourth-Quarter-Results/default.aspx)

## 实际结果

第四季度GAAP净销售额为[2134亿美元](https://ir.aboutamazon.com/news-release/news-release-details/2026/Amazon-com-Announces-Fourth-Quarter-Results/default.aspx)，同比增长14%；扣除汇率同比变化带来的28亿美元有利影响后，增长12%。[北美销售额](https://ir.aboutamazon.com/news-release/news-release-details/2026/Amazon-com-Announces-Fourth-Quarter-Results/default.aspx)为1271亿美元，同比+10%；[国际销售额](https://ir.aboutamazon.com/news-release/news-release-details/2026/Amazon-com-Announces-Fourth-Quarter-Results/default.aspx)507亿美元，同比+17%，扣除汇率后+11%；[AWS销售额](https://ir.aboutamazon.com/news-release/news-release-details/2026/Amazon-com-Announces-Fourth-Quarter-Results/default.aspx)356亿美元，同比+24%。

第四季度GAAP[营业利润](https://ir.aboutamazon.com/news-release/news-release-details/2026/Amazon-com-Announces-Fourth-Quarter-Results/default.aspx)为250亿美元，上年同期212亿美元。当季列报三项特别费用：意大利零售业务税务纠纷及一宗诉讼和解11亿美元、遣散费7.3亿美元、主要与实体店相关的资产减值6.1亿美元；公司口径剔除后营业利润为274亿美元。分部营业利润：北美115亿美元，国际10亿美元，AWS 125亿美元。GAAP净利润为212亿美元，摊薄后每股收益1.95美元；上年同期为200亿美元、1.86美元。

全年净销售额为[7169亿美元](https://ir.aboutamazon.com/news-release/news-release-details/2026/Amazon-com-Announces-Fourth-Quarter-Results/default.aspx)，同比+12%；营业利润800亿美元；净利润777亿美元，摊薄EPS 7.17美元。TTM经营现金流为[1395亿美元](https://ir.aboutamazon.com/news-release/news-release-details/2026/Amazon-com-Announces-Fourth-Quarter-Results/default.aspx)，同比+20%；TTM自由现金流112亿美元，上年同期382亿美元。下降主因是物业与设备购置净额同比增加507亿美元，公司称主要投向人工智能。
